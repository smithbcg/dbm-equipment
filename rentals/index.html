Try an XCMG Machine on Your Jobsite Before You Buy One

If your crew has not run an XCMG machine before, we can deliver one to your jobsite at no charge so your operators can try it on your own job. If it suits the work, we can put it on rent for you or sell it to you.

Every machine in our rental fleet is also for sale. Under our rental purchase option, if you buy the machine within six months, part of the rent you have paid goes toward the price. The sooner you buy, the larger that part is. Ask our sales team what share applies to a specific machine, or read how the option works on our Finance page.

Ask Our Sales Team About Buying
Inspected & photographed going out and coming back Our technicians inspect each rental before it leaves the yard. We photograph the machine when it goes out and when it comes back, so its condition is on record at both ends of the rental.
Jobsite delivery & pickup We truck the machine to your jobsite and pick it up when the rental ends. The quote lists the delivery charge beside the monthly rate, so you know both numbers before the machine leaves the yard.
Breakdowns on rent A DBM field technician repairs a broken-down rental where it sits on your jobsite. If we cannot fix it the same day, we prorate your rent, so you do not pay for the days the machine is down.
Monthly rental terms Most machines rent by the month. Large machines such as mining haul trucks can have a three-month minimum because of the cost of hauling them to and from the site. We rent to contractors and commercial operators, not to homeowners for weekend projects.
