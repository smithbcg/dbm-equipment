Sewer Pumps Lifted Into Place on a New Construction Site

Our crew and one of our crane trucks were already working on a customer’s new construction site when sewer pumps had to be lifted into place, so we lifted them in with the truck’s crane. Lifting pumps is not a service we sell, but when our crew and equipment are already on your site and can solve the problem in front of you, we solve it, and that customer still works with us today.

What Does DBM Do on a Job Site Every Day?

We repair machines of any make in the field, sell parts, rent machines by the month, and sell new XCMG and used equipment. The sewer pump lift happened because our crew and a crane truck were already on that site, and we do not sell lifting or crane services.
