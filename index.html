Parts and service

We carry parts and fix machines for XCMG, SAS, and most other makes.

Parts for XCMG, SAS, and most other machines

We carry XCMG and SAS parts, and we can get aftermarket parts for most other machines. We ship anywhere in the country.

How fast can I get a part?
Almost any part arrives within 48 hours, and we can ship it overnight when your machine is down.
How do I order a part?
Call or text (775) 625-0062, or send us the make, model, serial number, and the part you need. We confirm the price and how long it will take before we order it.
Can I get parts for a machine I didn’t buy from DBM?
Yes. We supply parts for machines bought anywhere, and we know the parts people at Caterpillar, Case, and other makers.

Field service and shop repair for any make

Our service trucks fix machines at the job site, the mine, or the quarry. If we sold you a machine in Nevada, we service it anywhere in the state.

How soon can someone get here?
Usually the day after you call, and sometimes the same day.
What do I use while my machine is being fixed?
We can put a rental machine on your site until yours is running again.
What if a DBM rental breaks down?
If we can’t fix it the same day, we take those days off your rent.
What if it’s after hours?
Send us a service request any time, and we’ll get back to you the next day.
